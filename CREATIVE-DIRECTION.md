# Stopzone promo creative direction

## Concept
"The city keeps moving. You don't have to watch it."

## Visual system
- Near-black background, warm white typography, vivid Stopzone red
- Motion built from route drawing, moving location marker, expanding wake-zone rings, dimensional phone movement and a controlled alert climax
- Avoid slideshow-like transitions and decorative motion without product meaning
- Clear, readable end card with official App Store CTA

## Suggested narration draft
"Ever looked up… and wondered if your stop is next?

Set your stop. Choose your wake zone.

Then put your phone away.

Stopzone tracks your distance while you ride.

And when it's time… you'll know.

Stopzone. Get off where you meant to.

Download Stopzone on the App Store today."
