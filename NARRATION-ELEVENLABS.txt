STOPZONE — ELEVENLABS TEXT-TO-SPEECH SCRIPT
Target duration: approximately 25–28 seconds.
Style: intimate, warm, modern, cinematic but natural. No exaggerated movie-trailer voice.
Voice: warm mid-low register, clear English, neutral international or natural US accent, moderate pace. Let the pauses do the work.

[softly]
Ever looked up… and wondered if your stop is next?

[pause]

Drop a pin. Set your zone.

Then lock your phone… and let the ride go by.

Stopzone tracks the miles while you rest.

[pause]

And when it’s time… [pause] it wakes you.

Stopzone. Get off where you meant to.

[excited]
Download Stopzone on the App Store today.

ELEVENLABS NOTES
- Try the Eleven v4 model if it is available in your account and supports the tags.
- Generate 2–3 takes with the same voice. Choose the most natural, assured performance rather than the most dramatic one.
- Keep the pauses: they are edit points for the route drawing, phone lock, and red wake-up reveal.
- If the model reads a tag aloud or ignores it, remove that tag and regenerate.
- Avoid adding [bell ringing] to the narration. The wake-up impact should be a separate sound effect in the edit.
- Say “Stopzone” as one word. If pronunciation is wrong, adjust voice instructions rather than changing the brand spelling.
- The final “App Store” CTA is for iOS. Verify the live listing and use the official App Store badge/link before publication.
