STOPZONE — ELEVENLABS TEXT-TO-SPEECH SCRIPT
Target duration: approximately 25–28 seconds
Delivery: natural, intimate, confident; modern premium product film, not a movie-trailer announcer.
Voice direction: mid-low register, warm and clear, neutral international English or natural US English. Moderate pace, close-mic feel. Underplay the opening, build warmth through the benefit, then land the brand and CTA with clarity.

[softly]
Ever looked up… and wondered if your stop is next?

[pause]

Drop a pin. Set your zone.

Then lock your phone… and let the ride go by.

Stopzone tracks the miles while you rest.

[pause]

And when it’s time… [pause] it wakes you.

[confident]
Stopzone. Get off where you meant to.

[slightly excited]
Download Stopzone on the App Store today.

PRODUCTION NOTES
- Generate 2–3 takes with the same voice. Keep the take that sounds human and assured, not over-acted.
- Use the ElevenLabs v4 model if available for audio-tag support. Tags are cues, not guaranteed effects.
- Keep the pauses; they create edit points for the route drawing, phone lock, and red alarm reveal.
- If ElevenLabs reads bracket tags aloud, remove tags unsupported by the selected model and generate again.
- Do not add sound-effect tags such as [bell ringing] inside this narration unless the generated sound is actually wanted in the voice track. Create the wake-up impact separately in the edit.
- Read “Stopzone” as one word. If the voice pronounces it incorrectly, try phonetic guidance in the voice prompt rather than altering the brand spelling in the visible copy.
- Final CTA uses “App Store” because the supplied end card is for iOS. Verify the live listing and use the official badge/link before publishing.
